# PRD outline

Use this as a starting point, not a mandatory section count. Omit irrelevant detail and preserve a supplied project template.

- **Problem and audience:** What is difficult today, who experiences it, and what evidence is available?
- **Outcome:** What observable improvement should result? Specify a metric only when its meaning and target are supported.
- **Scope:** Included behavior, explicit exclusions, and real constraints.
- **User flow:** Trigger, main actions, result, and consequential alternatives.
- **Acceptance scenarios:** For each requirement, give a stable identifier, precondition or trigger, expected behavior, and relevant failure or boundary cases. Keep expected results externally observable.
- **Open decisions:** Separate blocking questions from chosen assumptions. Record dependencies or rollout constraints only when known and relevant.

A compact scenario table may use `Requirement | Scenario | Expected result`. Do not invent Dev, QA, or production validation status. A proposed acceptance case is not an executed test.
